6. ADAS calibration and safe drive-away

Many vehicles have cameras or sensors mounted to the windshield that operate lane departure warning, automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise control. When a windshield is replaced, these systems generally require recalibration.

  • If your vehicle requires calibration and you decline it, those safety systems may not function as designed. You accept responsibility for that decision, and our warranty does not cover any resulting issues.
  • Calibration restores the system to the manufacturer's specification. It does not guarantee that a driver assistance feature will perform correctly in all conditions. These are aids, not substitutes for attentive driving.
  • Urethane adhesive needs time to cure. We will give you a safe drive-away time. Driving before then can compromise the installation and your safety, and is not covered by our warranty.
